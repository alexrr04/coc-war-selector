COC Clan War Selector

    Instructions:

    1. Enter the clan tag in the "Clan Tag" field.
    2. Select the minimum Town Hall level from the dropdown.
    3. Enter the number of participants (must be a multiple of 5).
    4. Click the "Select Warriors" button.
    5. Wait for the progress bar to complete and see the selected warriors listed below.
    6. Note that only the members of the clan with the war preference button set to IN will have the chance to appear in the lists. The war preference button can be found in your profile screen in Clash of Clans.
